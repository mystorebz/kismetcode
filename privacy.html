1. Introduction

Kismet Code Digital ("Kismet Code Digital," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of anyone who visits this website (kismetcodedigital.com, the "Site") or submits an inquiry through it. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the rights available to you under the laws of Belize, in particular the Data Protection Act, 2021 (Act No. 45 of 2021) ("the Act").

This Privacy Policy applies only to this Site. It does not apply to any third-party website you may reach through a link from this Site, or to any other service Kismet Code Digital may build for a client, which is instead governed by the privacy terms that apply to that client's own project.

2. Who is responsible for your data

For the purposes of the Act, Kismet Code Digital is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this Policy. Kismet Code Digital is a software development practice registered and operating in Belize. Contact details are provided in Section 19 below.

3. What personal data we collect

This Site is a static informational website. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect any personal data automatically simply by your browsing the Site. The only personal data we collect is the information you choose to submit through the contact form on the Contact page, specifically:

Data collected Why we ask for it
Your name So we know who we are corresponding with, and can address our reply to you.
Your email address So we can respond to your inquiry, and so we know where to send our reply.
Your phone number So we can contact you by phone if that is more convenient for discussing your project.
The type of project you are inquiring about, and your estimated budget So we can understand the general scope of what you are looking for before responding.
The description of your project So we can understand what you need before responding.

We do not knowingly collect any sensitive personal data (as that term is defined under the Act, including information about racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, genetic or biometric data, health, sexual orientation, financial position, or criminal record) through this Site, and we ask that you not include such information in your project description. If you go on to become a client, an engagement may involve additional information (such as billing details or, in some cases, information necessary to build a specific feature you have requested); any such information is covered by the terms agreed for that specific engagement rather than by this Policy, which describes only the Site itself.

4. How we use your personal data

We use the personal data described above solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss your project;
  • Provide you with a quote or proposal, if requested;
  • Keep a record of our correspondence with prospective clients, for our own reasonable business record-keeping; and
  • Where you go on to become a client, use your contact information for the ordinary purposes of delivering and invoicing that engagement.

We do not use the information you submit through the contact form for advertising, marketing to third parties, or for any purpose other than responding to your inquiry and, where applicable, delivering an engagement you have agreed to.

5. Our legal basis for processing

Under the Act, we may only process your personal data where we have a lawful basis to do so. When you submit the contact form, we process your personal data on the basis that:

  • You have given your consent to that processing, by voluntarily submitting the form; and
  • The processing is necessary for us to take steps, at your request, towards entering into a contract with you (namely, discussing and potentially quoting for the project you have described).

Where an inquiry becomes a paid engagement, our processing of your information for that engagement is based on the necessity of performing the contract between us.

Where you have submitted the form but have not become a client, you may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 19. Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of anything we did with your data before you withdrew it, but it means we will not use your information for any further purpose beyond what may already be necessary to wind down any existing correspondence.

6. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. The contact form on this Site is processed using EmailJS, a third-party service that delivers the content of your submission to our email inbox. EmailJS acts as a data processor on our behalf for this limited purpose: it receives the information you type into the form and forwards it to us by email, and does not use that information for its own purposes. You can review EmailJS's own privacy practices at emailjs.com.

Beyond this, we may disclose personal data where required to do so by law, by a court of competent jurisdiction, or to establish, exercise, or defend our legal rights, and, where you have become a client, to any subcontractor engaged to assist in delivering your engagement, subject to the confidentiality obligations described in our Terms of Service.

7. Cookies and tracking technologies

This Site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or any other tracking technology to monitor your browsing activity. We do not build a profile of visitors to this Site, and no data is collected about you simply by your visiting or browsing the Site. The only data we hold about you is what you choose to submit through the contact form, as described in Section 3.

If this changes in the future — for example, if we add website analytics to better understand how the Site is used — we will update this Policy accordingly before doing so, and will describe clearly what is collected and why.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. As a general guide:

Situation Typical retention period
Inquiry that does not lead to an engagement Deleted or anonymized within 12 months of the last correspondence, unless you request earlier deletion.
Inquiry that leads to an active engagement Retained for the duration of the engagement.
Records of a completed engagement (correspondence, invoices, project records) Retained for up to 7 years after completion, to meet ordinary business and tax record-keeping obligations under Belizean law.
Any personal data you ask us to delete under Section 12 Deleted without undue delay, except where we are required or permitted to retain it (for example, to comply with a legal obligation, or to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim).

These are general guidelines rather than fixed rules for every situation; the actual period we retain a given piece of information may vary depending on the nature of the inquiry or engagement and any specific legal obligation that applies.

9. How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal data submitted through this Site against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, consistent with the security obligations set out in the Act. This includes limiting access to inquiry correspondence to the people who need it to respond to you, and relying on EmailJS's own security measures for the transmission of form submissions. No method of transmission over the internet, however, is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information submitted to us.

10. Data breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach affecting your information, the Act requires us to notify the Data Protection Commissioner without undue delay, and, where the breach presents a high risk to your rights and freedoms, to notify you directly as well, within the timeframes set out in the Act. As a small practice, we do not expect to hold the kind of large-scale or sensitive personal data that would typically give rise to a high-risk breach, but we take this obligation seriously and will act promptly and transparently if a breach were to occur.

11. International data transfers

Because the contact form is processed using EmailJS, a third-party service, your personal data may be transferred to and processed on servers located outside of Belize. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, consistent with the requirements of the Act relating to transfers of personal data outside of Belize, including relying on the exemption available under the Act for transfers made for the purposes of cloud storage.

12. Your rights under Belizean law

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021, you have a number of rights in relation to the personal data we hold about you, including the right to:

  • Access — request confirmation of whether we are processing your personal data, and a copy of that data;
  • Rectification — request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you;
  • Erasure — request that we delete your personal data, where, for example, it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, or you withdraw consent and there is no other legal basis for us to keep it;
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your personal data in certain circumstances, such as while we verify its accuracy;
  • Object — object to our processing of your personal data where we are relying on legitimate interests as a legal basis, or to processing for direct marketing purposes; and
  • Data portability — request a copy of the personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, where technically feasible.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 19. We will respond to your request without undue delay, and in any event within the timeframes set out in the Act (ordinarily within one month of your request, extendable in more complex cases). We may ask you to verify your identity before responding to a request, to protect your information from being disclosed to the wrong person. These rights are provided to you free of charge, except where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, in which case we may charge a reasonable administrative fee or decline to act on the request, as permitted under the Act.

16. A note on the small business exemption

The Act includes an exemption for certain small businesses (broadly, those with annual turnover of BZD $3,000,000 or less in the previous financial year), subject to a number of exceptions that do not apply to what we do (such as providing health services, trading in personal information, or operating a credit reporting body). Kismet Code Digital provides this Privacy Policy, and applies the practices described in it, regardless of whether this exemption may technically apply to us, because we believe clients and visitors to this Site are entitled to clarity about how their information is handled.
